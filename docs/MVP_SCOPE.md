# MortgageMates MVP scope

## Pilot user

- First-time buyer, roughly 28–45
- Irish resident buying as an owner-occupier
- Open to exactly one unrelated co-buyer
- Looking in Greater Dublin or a commuter county
- Intends to buy within 3–18 months

## Core journey

1. Eligibility check
2. Account and six-step private profile
3. Document-readiness checklist and private vault
4. Admin review and one proposed match
5. Independent decision from each buyer
6. Mutual-interest identity and €49 gates
7. Contact unlock and alignment workbook
8. Consent-led preparation for independent broker and solicitor handoff

## Explicitly not in the MVP

- Property listings or search
- Swipe feeds or algorithmic self-serve browsing
- Mortgage, affordability, tax, or legal advice
- Bank or credit-bureau integrations
- Joint groups larger than two
- Legal document generation
- Shared access to the other buyer's raw documents
- Guaranteed mortgage or property outcomes

## Document-readiness boundary

“Accepted” means a pilot administrator has checked that the expected file appears present, current, and legible. It does not mean a broker, lender, solicitor, or government body has approved it. Checklist requirements are indicative and may change by professional, lender, employment type, and individual circumstances.
